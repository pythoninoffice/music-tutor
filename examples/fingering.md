# Axel F – piano fingering

1 = thumb, 2 = index, 3 = middle, 4 = ring, 5 = pinky. Key: F major / D minor.

## Left hand (bass)

- D3 = **1** (thumb), C3 = **3** (middle), A2 = **5** (pinky). Keep the hand resting over A2–D3.
- Pattern for each 4 bars (from the 2nd time through the main theme): D3 (whole) | D3 (whole) | A2 (half) C3 (half) | D3 (whole)

## Right hand (melody)

- **Main bar 1 – thumb on C4:** D4(2)  F4(4)  D4(2)  D4(2)  G4(5)  D4(2)  C4(1)
- **Main bar 2 – thumb on D4, stretch pinky to Bb:** D4(1)  A4(5)  D4(1)  D4(1)  Bb4(5)  A4(4)  F4(2)
- **Main bar 3 – jump up, then move thumb to A3:** D4(1)  A4(3)  D5(5)  D4(3)  C4(2)  C4(2)  A3(1)  E4(4)  D4(3)
- **Main bar 4 (keep holding the D4 from bar 3 for 2 beats, then):** D4(3)  D4(3)
- **Middle bars 1 & 3 – thumb on D4, chromatic walk down 5-4-3:** G4(5)  F#4(4)  F4(3)  F4(3)  E4(2)  E4(2)
- **Middle bar 2:** G4(5)  F#4(4)  F4(3)  F4(3)  E4(2)  E4(2)  E4(2)
- **Middle bar 4:** G4(5)  F#4(4)  F4(3)  F4(3)  D4(1)  D4(1)
- **Ending bar 3 (same as main bar 3):** D4(1)  A4(3)  D5(5)  D4(3)  C4(2)  C4(2)  A3(1)  E4(4)  D4(3)
- **Final note:** D5(3)

Order: main theme ×3 (1st time right hand alone, then with left hand), middle part ×2, ending (main bars 1–3, hold D4, then D5).
Tip: repeated notes (F4 F4, E4 E4, D4 D4) use the same finger – just lift and replay.
